Consumer Health Data Privacy Notice

How we handle food, body measurements, nutrition goals, and related information.

Effective and last updated November 23, 2026 · Version 2026-11-23.2

The updated beta is not open yet

The updated beta is not open to external testers. Self-service account deletion is available in the latest development build. Account syncing and AI processing remain paused pending activation and separate, current permissions. Scheduled recovery of interrupted deletions and automatic photo, AI-response, and usage cleanup are not yet running. Earlier prototype users can contact us now to request access, withdrawal, or deletion. Automatic 30-day photo cleanup has not started.

Scope and operators

James White and Jonathan Zhou jointly operate KcalPal from Texas. This separate notice supplements the general privacy notice and explains our consumer health data practices. The beta is intended for invited US adults aged 18 or older. Privacy rights vary by applicable law; this notice does not limit those rights.

Food logs and body information can reveal or support inferences about health even when you use the app for general wellness. KcalPal provides nutrition estimates, not medical diagnosis or treatment.

Categories of data and their sources

  • Nutrition and eating information: foods and drinks, amounts, calories, macronutrients, meal times and categories, recipes, photos, saved meals, and food-related messages you submit.
  • Body, activity, and goals: age, height, weight, activity, the sex-related calculation input, and personal calorie, macro, or weight-related goals you choose to enter.
  • Derived nutrition information: estimated intake, suggested targets, and meal patterns or remembered-meal context derived from your entries, calculations, and AI processing.
  • Identifiers associated with that information: account identifiers and email, and relevant request or technical records needed to provide, protect, and troubleshoot the service.

Sources are you, your prior KcalPal records, on-device calculations, AI output, and nutrition reference information from restaurant research, USDA FoodData Central, and Open Food Facts. We do not obtain clinical records from your healthcare provider. Please do not submit medical records or another person's personal information.

Why we collect and use it

We use this information to provide the features you request: record and correct meals, show totals and history, save recipes, retrieve usual meals, and suggest goals. Relevant information is also used to synchronize permitted account records, recover interrupted work, prevent duplicate changes, secure the service, control usage, and respond to your requests.

We do not sell consumer health data, use it for advertising, or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. KcalPal does not use location-based geofences around healthcare facilities.

What is shared and with whom

Authorized KcalPal operators can access account records for the operational and support purposes described above. The recipient categories are infrastructure/storage providers, AI and nutrition-reference providers, email/support services, and the beta-distribution provider:

  • Supabase: account information, food and profile records, recipes, uploaded photos, and related operational records for authentication, storage, and processing.
  • OpenAI: the messages, selected photos, and relevant account food history or saved recipes needed for AI requests. Restaurant web research uses derived search terms. OpenAI's separate safety and retention practices are explained in the general notice.
  • USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts: food search terms and barcodes for reference nutrition. Account email and account identifiers are not intentionally included; descriptions can still contain information you provide.
  • Google/Gmail: email contents and addressing when we send sign-in codes or you contact us, including health information you choose to put in correspondence.
  • Apple: TestFlight usage, crash information, and feedback. Feedback or screenshots you choose to submit may contain food or other personal information. Apple's TestFlight practices apply.

These are the providers identified for the beta; their operational roles and independent privacy practices differ. We may make a disclosure required by a valid legal obligation, subject to applicable law. Visiting this public policy website sends ordinary visitor information to GitHub, but does not upload your KcalPal food log or photos.

Choices, requests, and appeals

When account access resumes, the updated beta will ask separately for account storage and AI sharing. Providing questionnaire information is optional. Once access is enabled, the latest development build's Your data & choices controls will let you grant or withdraw sharing permissions. Account deletion is already available in that build while syncing and AI remain paused. You can also contact us now to request withdrawal or deletion. Withdrawal stops future permitted processing but cannot recall information already transmitted.

You may ask to confirm whether we collect, use, or share your consumer health data; obtain access or a copy and information about recipients; correct information; withdraw consent; or delete it. We will handle requests and any permitted exceptions under applicable law. Some features require the information you ask us to stop using.

Email jbw2cs@gmail.com, preferably from your account's email address. We will verify the request proportionately. Never send passwords, email codes, recovery tokens, or government ID in ordinary email. We will respond within applicable legal time limits. If a request is denied, we will explain why and how to appeal; send an appeal to the same address with “Privacy appeal” in the subject. You can also contact your state attorney general or relevant regulator. We will not retaliate against you for exercising applicable privacy rights.

Retention and deletion

Account records currently remain until removed through an available control or a verified request. Removing a food entry or recipe hides its active entry while retaining a synchronization record; related conversations can remain. Account deletion and narrower privacy requests are separate from removing a visible item. Self-service deletion is available in the latest development build. Interrupted requests remain resumable, but scheduled server recovery is not yet running: reopen the app and use Continue deletion, or contact us for help.

We selected 30-day uploaded-photo expiry for the updated beta, with a full 30-day rollout grace for existing photos. It has not been activated. Saved food and recipe text will not be deleted by photo expiry. Prepared schedules also remove completed temporary AI response content after 30 days and detailed usage after 90 days once enabled. The general retention section explains these schedules and retained request/deletion records.

Mailbox copies remain until manually deleted; there is no fixed automatic email-retention schedule yet. Device backups and provider security or legal records are separate. We cannot promise immediate erasure of all provider systems or offline devices. Requests will be assessed for the deletion and provider-notification duties that apply; limited retention exceptions must have an applicable basis.
